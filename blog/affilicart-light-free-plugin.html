If you're an Amazon Associates creator, you already know the struggle: readers click one product link and bounce away before seeing the rest of your recommendations.

The good news? You don't need to spend a dime to fix this problem.

AffiliCart Light is a fully functional, free version of the plugin that gives you a real on-site shopping cart experience — no upsells, no limited trials, and no risk.

What You Get with AffiliCart Light (It's Surprisingly Powerful)

Even in the free version, AffiliCart delivers serious value for Amazon affiliate sites:

  • Full Shopping Cart Experience — Readers can add multiple products to a persistent cart that works across your entire site.
  • One-Click Checkout on Amazon — When they're ready, one button sends everything to Amazon with your affiliate tag automatically attached.
  • Powerful Shortcodes — Easily display product grids, single "Add to Cart" buttons, or hover preview links anywhere on your posts and pages.
  • Auto-Tagging — Every Amazon link (including shortened a.co links) gets your affiliate ID added automatically. Never lose a commission again.
  • Unlimited Products — Add as many products as you want. No limits on carts or items.
  • Responsive Design — Works beautifully on mobile and desktop.
  • ASIN Copier Chrome Extension — Free companion tool to instantly grab product ASINs from Amazon.

You can start publishing professional-looking product displays and multi-item carts today — completely free.

Real Benefits You'll Notice Immediately
