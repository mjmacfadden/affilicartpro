Introduction

Amazon Associates earn commissions on everything in the customer's cart—not just the clicked product. Yet most affiliate sites still use single links. AffiliCart changes the game with a true multi-product cart.

How the AffiliCart Cart Actually Works

  1. Readers see your product grids or buttons → click "Add to Cart" → floating cart icon updates in real time.
  2. When they click "Checkout on Amazon", every item is pre-loaded in their Amazon cart and your affiliate tag is attached to the entire order.

Proven Benefits

Lower bounce rates – Readers stay on your site comparing options instead of leaving immediately after viewing one product.
Higher average order value – One-click multi-item checkout encourages add-ons and impulse buying.
Better user experience – Feels like shopping on Amazon itself, increasing trust and repeat visits.
Compliant & future-proof – No live pricing until you qualify for Amazon API access (AffiliCart handles this gracefully).
